About us

A Wah Cantt agency built around one Gulf-bound trip at a time.

Khaleej Travels started with a simple frustration: families arranging Umrah were dealing with one agent for flights, another for visas, and a separate office for hotel bookings. We put all three under one roof, so a single conversation on WhatsApp covers the whole trip.

Today we handle Umrah packages, Qatar and KSA work visas, and flight ticketing for individuals, families, and groups — with the same person following your booking from first message to departure.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Khaleej Travels ("we", "us") arranges Umrah packages, flight tickets, and work visa processing for customers in Pakistan. This explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use the inquiry form on this site, we collect the details you choose to provide: your name, phone number, the service you're interested in, and any message or travel details you enter.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry with package options, pricing, or visa/ticketing guidance.
  • To follow up with you by WhatsApp, phone, or email about your request.
  • To prepare quotes, bookings, and travel or visa documentation if you proceed with our services.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties except as described below.

How your inquiry is sent

Submitting the contact form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to our team with your details, which you then send to us directly from your own WhatsApp account.

Sharing with service providers

If you proceed with a booking, we may share necessary details (such as your name and travel dates) with airlines, hotels, or visa processing authorities strictly to arrange your travel or visa.

Data retention

We keep inquiry and booking details for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet our own record-keeping or legal obligations, after which we delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it, by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing us using the contact details above.
